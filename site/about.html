ABOUT / THE SHORT VERSION

Follow the detail.
Keep asking why.

Fieldnotes is an independent collection of writing about software, systems, and how we make sense of both.

Built around a question

What changes when we look a little closer? A cache directive becomes a decision about ownership. A hidden element reveals the difference between a document and its layout. A regional percentile opens a conversation about what a measurement can actually establish.

This journal follows those threads through articles, working notes, and practical guides. Each article links to its source material, and earlier notes retain their publication dates so their assumptions can be read in context.

Read with the references open

A technical claim deserves more than a confident sentence. Compare the wording of an article with the primary source, check the version, and distinguish an observation from its explanation. Some notes preserve incomplete or conflicting accounts; their references are a starting point for resolving the difference.

A small, open collection

The journal’s editorial contributors are fictional. The collection is self-contained, uses no remote fonts or analytics, and works as a set of ordinary HTML documents. Saved articles remain on the reader’s device. Search runs entirely in the browser.

Begin with the quiet architecture of a faster web, or browse the source library.
